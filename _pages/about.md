I am a water resources researcher and consultant based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, currently with **EQMS Consulting Limited**. My work spans two connected tracks: climate science research on marine heatwaves, Time of Emergence analysis, and CMIP6 projections, and applied hydrology and flood risk consulting on ADB- and BEZA-funded infrastructure projects in Bangladesh.

I hold an M.Sc. in Water Resources Development from the Institute of Water and Flood Management (IWFM), BUET, where my thesis examined the spatiotemporal warming level and Time of Emergence of climate extremes in Bangladesh using ERA5 and CMIP6 data. I previously worked as an Associate Water Resources Engineer at EIMS Ltd., leading hydrological modelling and anticipatory action trigger development for flash floods, droughts, and cyclones.

I currently serve as an **Expert Reviewer for the IPCC AR7 Report (First Order Draft)**.

My research interests include hydrologic and hydraulic modelling, uncertainty quantification, flood and drought risk assessment, compound hydroclimatic extremes, CMIP6 multi-model ensemble analysis, statistical climate signal detection, and anticipatory action for climate risk.
